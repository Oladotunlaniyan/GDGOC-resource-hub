# 🎯 Job Hunting & Resume Guide

Landing your dream role in tech requires more than just coding skills. It takes strategy, persistence, and the right tools.

---

## 🤔 What is Job Hunting?

Job hunting is the proactive process of seeking employment opportunities. It's not just about applying to open positions; it's a strategic campaign to market your skills and value to potential employers.

It involves:
- **Self-Discovery:** Understanding your strengths, interests, and career goals.
- **Market Research:** Identifying companies and roles that align with your aspirations.
- **Networking:** Building relationships with professionals in your field.
- **Personal Branding:** Showcasing your expertise through your resume, portfolio, and online presence.
- **Application & Interviewing:** Submitting tailored applications and demonstrating your fit during interviews.

> [!NOTE]
> Job hunting is a marathon, not a sprint. Consistency and resilience are key.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Needed for Job Hunting

Equip yourself with the right tools to streamline your search and stand out.

### Essential Documents
- **Resume:** Your primary marketing document. Keep it concise (1 page usually), ATS-friendly, and tailored to the job description.
- **Cover Letter:** A personalized narrative that connects your experience to the company's needs.
- **Portfolio:** A showcase of your projects and code (GitHub, personal website).

### Digital Platforms
- **LinkedIn:** For networking, job alerts, and researching companies. Optimize your profile!
- **GitHub:** To demonstrate your coding activity and contributions.
- **Job Boards:** LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, Glassdoor, Wellfound (formerly AngelList), RemoteOK.

### Organization Tools
- **Spreadsheet/Notion:** Track your applications (Company, Role, Date Applied, Status, Notes).
- **Calendar:** Schedule interviews and follow-up reminders.

## 🎨 The Art of the Hunt: Be Creative

Don't just follow the herd. Stand out by bringing creativity and personality to your job search.

### 1. The "Proof of Work" Approach
Instead of just telling them you can do the job, **show them**.
- **Build a mini-project** related to the company's product.
- **Write a blog post** analyzing their tech stack or a recent feature.
- **Fix a bug** in their open-source repositories.

### 2. Network with Value
Don't just ask for a referral. Offer value first.
- Engage with their content on LinkedIn/Twitter.
- Share thoughtful insights or resources.
- Attend their events and ask good questions.

### 3. Storytelling
Your resume lists facts, but you are a story.
- **Craft your "Elevator Pitch":** Who are you, what do you do, and why does it matter?
- **STAR Method:** In interviews, tell stories using **S**ituation, **T**ask, **A**ction, **R**esult.

### 4. Mindset Matters
- **Rejection is Redirection:** Every "no" brings you closer to the right "yes".
- **Continuous Learning:** Use the downtime to upskill.
- **Stay Curious:** Ask questions about the company culture and challenges.
